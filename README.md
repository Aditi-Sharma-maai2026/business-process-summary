# AI Meets Human-Centric Processes in Service: A Comparative Study of Sephora (USA) vs. Matas Group (Scandinavia)

## Executive Summary

Contemporary retail performance is increasingly defined by the end-to-end quality of the customer experience rather than product assortment or pricing alone. In beauty retail, where purchase decisions rely heavily on expert judgment, high subjectivity, and personal trust, integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) presents a distinct operational dilemma:

**Should AI serve as a frontline substitute for human advice, or as a backend enabler that supports human expertise?**

This study addresses this challenge through a comparative analysis of two market leaders:

- **Sephora (USA):** Pursues a **technology-led, front-end AI integration** model anchored in Business Process Re-engineering (BPR) and Voice of Process (VOP) efficiency.
- **Matas Group (Scandinavia):** Employs a **human-centered, back-end AI integration** model anchored in Total Quality Management (TQM) and Voice of Customer (VOC) trust.

By applying identical Business Process Excellence (BPE) and Lean Six Sigma (LSS) toolkits across both firms, this research demonstrates how organizational culture, customer expectations, and digital maturity dictate operational outcomes.

### Data Triangulation

1. **Primary Data:** 7 semi-structured interviews across store advisors (N=5) and store/managing managers (N=2) at Sephora and Matas Group.
2. **Secondary Data:** Corporate disclosures and annual reports (2016–2025), industry benchmark metrics, press releases on AI/AR deployments, and peer-reviewed BPE/LSS literature.

## Strategic & Operational Comparison

| Analytical Dimension | Sephora (USA) | Matas Group (Scandinavia) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Primary Philosophy** | **Tech-Assisted / BPR** (Automation & Standardization) | **Human-Assisted / TQM** (Empowerment & Continuous Improvement) |
| **Front-Stage Flow** | AI/AR virtual try-on & Color iQ diagnosis precedes human contact. | Human greeting, dialogue, and manual testing supported by CRM data. |
| **Back-Stage Flow** | Automated query routing, digital triage, and Kanban escalation. | Real-time AI inventory management & automated logistics hubs. |
| **BPE Value Focus** | **Voice of Process (VOP):** Defect reduction, cycle time, standardization. | **Voice of Customer (VOC):** Relational depth, empathy, trust, long-term CSAT. |
| **Key COPQ / Defects** | Rework via product returns; high variation in subjective human advice. | Operational bottlenecks from manual stock checks; inconsistent advisor knowledge. |
| **Quantified Outcomes** | **~30% reduction in returns** post-AI implementation. | Reduced stockroom motion waste; stabilized sales-floor availability. |
| **Primary Risk Vectors** | Complex case bottlenecks; CCPA & EU AI Act regulatory exposure. | Regional service variation; language limits (Danish-only bot); inclusion gaps. |

## Process Analysis & Applied Frameworks

### 1. In-Store Consultation & Product Recommendation

#### Sephora - BPR Approach

Shifts initial shade diagnosis upstream to AI/AR tools. By standardizing advice before human interaction occurs, Sephora targets the **waste of rework** (returns from incorrect shade matches) and reduces advisor-to-advisor process variance.

#### Matas Group - TQM Approach

Retains human advisors ("materialists") as central decision-makers. AI is applied to backend inventory tracking to eliminate:

- **Waste of Motion:** Searching stockrooms for products.
- **Waste of Waiting:** Delays caused by manual stock checks.

This allows advisors to spend maximum time delivering high-trust consultations.

### 2. Complex Problem Resolution & Returns Management

#### Sephora

- Uses low-touch, rule-based digital triage.
- Routine queries are resolved via automated chatbots.
- Complex health/shade exceptions use **Kanban-like signals** to route cases to specialized human teams.

#### Matas Group

- Uses high-touch, advisor-led shop-floor resolution.
- Staff leverage historical CRM purchase data alongside **Simon’s Bounded Rationality framework** to make empathetic, context-driven return decisions on the spot.

### 3. Ishikawa (Fishbone) Root Cause Analysis

Cross-process root causes were mapped across six core operational axes:

| Category | Identified Root Causes |
|:---|:---|
| **People** | Skill variation in staff advice; inconsistent diversity/neurodiversity training. |
| **Material** | Tester/sample quality variance; backend stock availability visibility gaps. |
| **Method** | Non-standardized consultation protocols; long decision loops in returns. |
| **Technology** | System integration delays (HR/CRM); device/lighting dependencies in AR matching; Danish-only chatbot limitations. |
| **Environment** | Store layouts constraining customer flow; varying regional service expectations (Nordic vs. Global). |

## Redesign Proposals & Governance (IMPROVE & CONTROL)

### Process Redesign Proposals

#### Sephora Redesign

Integrates an **AI Confidence Score Threshold** during virtual try-on.

- Human advisors step in *only* when the AI confidence score falls below a set threshold.
- Eliminates unnecessary staff intervention while protecting recommendation accuracy.

#### Matas Group Redesign

Introduces a **Standardized 4-Question Critical-to-Quality (CTQ) Assessment** backed by a lightweight AI suggestion tool.

- Recommendations and customer preferences are automatically logged into the CRM.
- Builds organizational memory and improves consistency across stores.

### Governance & Control Framework (KPI Balance)

#### Sephora Control Plan

- Shade-Match Accuracy (%)
- Consultation Cycle Time (s)
- AI System Adoption per Store
- Color-Related Return Rates

#### Matas Group Control Plan

- CTQ Assessment Compliance (%)
- Staff Training Completion Index
- Store-to-Store CSAT Variance
- Consultation Rework Rates

---

## Research Contribution

This comparative study demonstrates that successful AI integration in service operations does not require replacing human expertise with automation.

Instead, the optimal approach depends on the organization's strategic priorities:

- **Sephora:** AI as a frontline efficiency and standardization enabler.
- **Matas Group:** AI as a backend operational support system that protects human-centered service.

**The central finding:** AI creates the greatest value in beauty retail when its implementation aligns with organizational culture, customer expectations, and the specific operational context.
